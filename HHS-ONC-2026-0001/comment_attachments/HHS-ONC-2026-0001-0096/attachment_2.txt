Health Command & Avatar Tethering Framework
A State-Led Model for Responsible AI Integration in Clinical Care
Submitted in Response to: HHS RFI — Accelerating the Adoption and Use of Artificial
Intelligence in Clinical Care
Executive Overview & National Relevance
Purpose
This brief presents the Health Command Intermediary AI Framework, currently under
legislative consideration in Alabama (SB97) and Kentucky (SB175), as a structured, state-
governed model for responsible AI deployment in clinical care. The system is designed to align
with HHS objectives concerning:
• Regulatory clarity
• Payment modernization
• Rural health sustainability
• Workforce augmentation
• Research and implementation science
Health Command integrates a patented methodology known as Avatar Tethering, which
enhances contextual care coordination and sustainable engagement within an AI-supported
healthcare ecosystem.
The National Challenge
Rural healthcare systems face accelerating pressures:
• Physician and specialist shortages
• Uncompensated emergency department utilization
• Aging populations
• Payment model inefficiencies
• Increasing telehealth competition from out-of-state providers
Simultaneously, AI innovation is advancing rapidly without uniform governance structures or
value-aligned reimbursement pathways.
A scalable, governance-based framework is required to prevent fragmented adoption and
unintended consequences.

Health Command: System Architecture
Health Command operates as a non-autonomous AI intermediary platform that:
• Performs triage and care navigation
• Supports chronic disease monitoring
• Routes patients to licensed providers
• Assists workforce documentation and workflow
• Integrates telehealth escalation pathways
Licensed clinicians retain authority for diagnosis, prescribing, and treatment decisions.
The system is structured to operate under state oversight and in compliance with federal
privacy, civil rights, and interoperability standards.

Avatar Tethering Sustainability Model
Avatar Tethering allows contextual linking of:
• Patients
• Licensed providers
• Institutions
• Educational partners
• Approved sponsor entities
This model creates modest, scalable revenue support for rural healthcare institutions without
increasing patient out-of-pocket costs.
Revenue mechanisms are structured for transparency and state oversight to avoid inducement
or volume-based distortions.
