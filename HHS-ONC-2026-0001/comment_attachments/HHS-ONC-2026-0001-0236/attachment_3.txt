Governance templates

Template: risk-tier classification for non-device clinical Al (minimum
viable)

Risk tier determined by:

¢ Context of use (administrative vs care operations vs clinical decision influence)

* Reversibility of harm and time sensitivity

* Degree of autonomy (recommendation vs action execution)

* Data sensitivity and longitudinal aggregation requirements

¢ Equity sensitivity (risk of disparate impact)

¢ Auditability and traceability availability

* Tie risk analysis dimensions to Predictive DSI IRM categories (validity, reliability, robustness, fairness,
intelligibility, safety, security, privacy).

Model contract principles (non-device clinical Al)

These are baseline principles; parties should adapt to context.

1. Auditability and logs: access to logs sufficient to investigate incidents and reproduce outputs used in care.
2. Version transparency: disclosure of model/version changes; notice of material updates; change logs.

3. Monitoring obligations: define vendor and deployer responsibilities for drift detection and safety monitoring.

4. Purpose fidelity and secondary use boundaries: explicit permitted uses of PHI and non-PHI; no implied
training/secondary use beyond agreed scope; align with HIPAA permitted uses and disclosures.

5. Interoperability and exit: open standards compliance, data portability, and transition support; align to
USCDI/FHIR baseline.

6. Non-discriminatory access: avoid pricing and technical practices that create de facto lock-in or capture
through unusable friction; align to information blocking posture and exceptions framework.
